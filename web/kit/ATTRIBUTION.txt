The drum kit served next to the editor (the Drum kit page's preloaded pads, the Songs tab's player):

MuldjordKit by Lars Muldjord and the DrumGizmo team (https://www.drumgizmo.org), a Tama Superstar kit, from the
SFZ port https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.MuldjordKit (mapped by kinwie), licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Two sounds a lane where it matters: the hardest velocity layer
(LANE_note.wav, the device's USR3) and a softer one (LANE_note-soft.wav, USR4: ghost and soft hits), the first round
robin, its microphones mixed down, trimmed, cut and levelled by tools/mkkit.py (mono 16-bit WAV at 22050 Hz; the
closed and pedal hats and the rim at 44100 Hz).
Lanes: KICK, KICK2, SNARE, RIM, SNARE2, LOWTOM, MIDTOM (Tom2, 2026-10-14: the lane was the conga's), HITOM (Tom1), CRASH,
SHAKER (a splash).

RIDE: AVL Drumkits "Red Zeppelin" (a Zildjian A 24" ride) and HAT, OPENHAT and PEDAL: AVL Drumkits "Black Pearl", both by Glen MacArthur (Bandshed / AV Linux,
http://www.bandshed.net/avldrumkits/), licensed CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
the same way (the hardest layer, trimmed, cut, levelled). These four files carry that licence.

CLAP and COWBELL: SLOOP's CC0 studio recordings (assets/samples-cc0/KIT: Versilian Studios VSCO-2 CE
and VCSL, Sonic Pi), the same way.
