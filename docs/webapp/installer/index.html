Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

A drummer for guitarists: your songs as sections (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, solo, outro) that you switch live from your pedalboard over the TRS MIDI jack, with fills where you ask for them. No backing tracks: how long each section lasts is decided on stage.

16songs, up to 16 sections each
16factory grooves, ready to play
4velocity layers a drum: the muldjord kit
trsmidi in: cc and pc from your pedalboard
